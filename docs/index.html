House price explorer

Held-out test predictions with 90% prediction intervals, plus model comparison, error by price segment and SHAP importance. Everything is precomputed by the repository's train and evaluate runs; nothing is fitted in the browser.

Research demo on old, small public data. Not a valuation or appraisal tool.
